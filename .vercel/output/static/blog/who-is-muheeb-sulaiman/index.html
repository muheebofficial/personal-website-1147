Who is Muheeb Sulaiman?

The internet is going agog! Pandemonium everywhere!! 😂 People are wondering. People are asking: “Is Muheeb AI?” “Is Muheeb an AI tool?” “Or is he just someone that makes use of AI?”

Well, in this video—sorry, post rather, I want to clear the air on who or what Muheeb Sul-AI-man really is.

Let’s explore. 👀

First, let’s establish the facts. Muheeb can write, Muheeb can build Website Design & Conversion Funnels, Muheeb can run Google Ads, Muheeb can work with AIO, AEO, GEO, SEO & Organic Growth, Muheeb can develop strategies, Muheeb can automate processes with AI Automation & Workflow Optimisation, and, increasingly, Muheeb can make AI do a lot of the heavy lifting. Google’s Search Central documentation is a useful external reference for the search and content work described here.

Now, here is where things get interesting.

If I use AI to research faster, does that make me AI?, If I use AI to write better, does that make the writing “AI”?, If I use AI to automate a business process, who gets the credit? me the the human or the machine? and if I build systems that allow AI agents to perform tasks that humans traditionally do, at what point do you stop saying “he uses AI” and start saying “he is an AI guy”? 🤔. Maybe that’s the real question.

Because I don’t think the uninteresting part is whether Muheeb is AI. The interesting part is what Muheeb is doing with AI.

AI isn’t replacing my thinking, It’s expanding what I can think about, build, test and execute. It’s not the destination, It’s leverage.

So, is Muheeb Sul-AI-man AI?

I’ll leave that question open for now. 😂

But one thing is becoming increasingly difficult to deny: The way I work is changing and AI is a very big part of that change and honestly, we’re only getting started. 🚀
